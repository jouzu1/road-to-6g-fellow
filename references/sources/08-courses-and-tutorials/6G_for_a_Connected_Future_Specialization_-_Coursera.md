#### Specialization - 3 course series

This Specialization is intended for learners who want to explore the cutting-edge advancements shaping the future of wireless communication in the 6G era. You will cover key enabling technologies such as Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS), LiFi, Open RAN (O-RAN), Blockchain, IoT, Robotics, and AI-driven communications. You will also learn about advanced topics, including semantic communications, integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), and 6G-enabled healthcare applications. By engaging with insights from leading experts and following a structured approach spanning foundations, core innovations, and future research challenges, you will gain the knowledge and skills needed to contribute meaningfully to the evolving 6G landscape. You can watch our course trailer video for the 6G for a Connected Future Specialization on [YouTube](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHlffP3Ir60>) . **Applied Learning Project** Learners will build knowledge in the fundamental concepts and key technologies driving 6G, including AI-enabled intelligent networks and their applications in healthcare, smart cities, and autonomous systems. They will develop the ability to critically analyse cutting-edge research and industry insights to understand how 6G will transform wireless connectivity. Additionally, they will gain awareness of the challenges, sustainability considerations, and future directions shaping next-generation wireless technologies. Show more

#### Frequently asked questions

##### How long does it take to complete the Specialization?

14 weeks of study, 4-5 hours/week

##### What background knowledge is necessary?

A basic understanding of wireless communication, networking, and signal processing is recommended, but not mandatory. Familiarity with 5G concepts, AI, and IoT will be beneficial for deeper engagement. The course will also be of interest to those in machine learning, cybersecurity, and digital transformation who wish to understand how their fields intersect with 6G development.

##### Do I need to take the courses in a specific order?

No, you don't need to take the courses in a specific order, but we recommend following Part 1, Part 2, then Part 3 for a progressive learning experience.

##### Will I earn university credit for completing the Specialization?

Not applicable at this stage.

##### What will I be able to do upon completing the Specialization?

Upon completing the 6G Emerging Technologies Specialization, you will be able to understand and apply fundamental and advanced 6G concepts, including AI-driven and IoT-enabled networks. You will analyze cutting-edge research, design and evaluate smart radio and optical environments, and explore blockchain, semantic communications, and multimodal networks. Additionally, you will gain practical skills in digital twin communications, advanced antenna design, and testbed development, preparing you to address real-world deployment challenges and contribute to the evolution of next-generation wireless technologies.

##### Is this course really 100% online? Do I need to attend any classes in person?

This course is completely online, so there's no need to show up to a classroom in person. You can access your lectures, readings and assignments anytime and anywhere via the web or your mobile device.

##### Can I just enroll in a single course?

Yes! To get started, click the course card that interests you and enroll. You can enroll and complete the course to earn a shareable certificate. When you subscribe to a course that is part of a Specialization, you're automatically subscribed to the full Specialization. Visit your learner dashboard to track your progress.

##### Is financial aid available?

Yes. In select learning programs, you can apply for financial aid or a scholarship if you can't afford the enrollment fee. If fin aid or scholarship is available for your learning program selection, you'll find a link to apply on the description page.

##### Can I take the course for free?

No, you cannot take this course for free. When you enroll in the course, you get access to all of the courses in the Specialization, and you earn a certificate when you complete the work. If you cannot afford the fee, you can apply for financial aid. Show all 9 frequently asked questions
