[MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW)](<https://ocw.mit.edu/index.htm?utm_source=openlearning&utm_medium=ocwpage>) is a free, publicly accessible, openly-licensed digital collection of high-quality teaching and learning materials, presented in an easily accessible format. Browse through, download and use materials from more than 2,500 MIT on-campus courses and supplemental resources, all available under a [Creative Commons license](<http://ocw.mit.edu/terms/?utm_source=open%20learning&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=landing%20page#cc>) for open sharing. Complementing these course materials, OCW also offers: [OCW Educator A portal where MIT instructors share special publications describing how they teach with their OCW materials, in this way helping education professionals find and use OCW content in their classrooms. Learn more](<https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?f=Instructor%20Insights&s=-runs.best_start_date>) [Chalk Radio Podcast Conversations with MIT faculty about inspired teaching at MIT. Go behind the scenes in some of the most interesting courses, from nuclear physics to film appreciation to hands-on digital design. Learn more](<https://chalk-radio.simplecast.com/>) [OCW YouTube Channel Millions of learners discover OCW and MIT Open Learning through videos shared on YouTube's most-subscribed .edu channel. Learn more](<https://www.youtube.com/c/mitocw>) [Explore MIT OCW](<https://ocw.mit.edu/index.htm?utm_source=openlearning&utm_medium=ocwpage>)
