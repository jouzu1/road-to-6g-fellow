#### What is 6G?

6G is the sixth generation of mobile network technology, defined by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) under the IMT-2030 framework to enable ultra-high-speed, intelligent, and ubiquitous connectivity beyond the capabilities of 5G. It integrates advanced radio access, AI-native networking, and sensing capabilities into a unified system designed for immersive experiences, mission-critical applications, and global coverage. The 6G system comprises several core components: 6G Radio (6GR) for terahertz and sub-terahertz spectrum use, 6G Radio Access Network (6G RAN) supporting ultra-massive MIMO and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, 6G Core Network (6GC) enabling cloud-native and AI-driven operations, Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) for real-time environmental mapping, and Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN) for satellite-based connectivity. These components work together to deliver extreme performance, pervasive intelligence, and seamless interoperability across terrestrial and aerial domains. 6G is designed for network architects, systems engineers, telecom operators, and R&D professionals who are shaping next-generation communication systems. These practitioners benefit from its native AI integration, ultra-low latency, and sensing capabilities to build intelligent, secure, and sustainable networks that support advanced applications in industries such as autonomous mobility, smart cities, and industrial automation.
