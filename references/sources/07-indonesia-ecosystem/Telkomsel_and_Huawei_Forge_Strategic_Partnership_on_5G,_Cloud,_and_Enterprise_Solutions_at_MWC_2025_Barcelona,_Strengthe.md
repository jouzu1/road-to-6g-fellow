### Telkomsel and Huawei Forge Strategic Partnership on 5G, Cloud, and Enterprise Solutions at MWC 2025 Barcelona, Strengthening Joint Innovation and Digital Talent Development

04 March 2025 Telkomsel dan Huawei Jalin Kemitraan Strategis dalam 5G, Cloud, dan Solusi Enterprise di MWC 2025 Barcelona **BARCELONA, Spain, March 3, 2025** – PT Telekomunikasi Selular (Telkomsel) and PT Huawei Tech Investment (Huawei Indonesia) have officially established a strategic partnership to accelerate digital business growth in 5G, Cloud, and Enterprise Solutions. Officially signed by both parties at Mobile World Congress (MWC) 2025 Barcelona, the collaboration focuses on two key aspects: joint innovation in digital technology development, and talent development programs to enhance the competencies of the telecommunications workforce in Indonesia. The signing ceremony was attended by Indra Mardiatna, Chief Technology Officer at Telkomsel, and Li Bingyao, Director of Huawei Indonesia Delivery and Service Business Department. Under this one-year agreement, Telkomsel and Huawei will collaborate on two main initiatives: **1\. Joint Innovation in Digital Technology** a. Developing technological solutions to improve network coverage and service quality, especially in densely populated areas. b. Optimizing infrastructure investments to create a more efficient network with sufficient capacity to deliver an enhanced 5G experience. c. Utilizing the latest technologies to enhance digital services, including VoLTE-based communication solutions and high-quality video and streaming services. d. Exploring the application of digital technologies in industrial and agricultural sectors to boost productivity through smarter connectivity. **2\. Digital Talent Development** a. Conducting training programs and knowledge transfer initiatives to ensure telecommunications workforce can effectively adopt the latest technologies. b. Strengthening workforce skills in digital infrastructure management, cloud technology utilization, and AI-based solution implementation. c. Preparing Indonesia's digital talent to be more equipped for the evolving telecommunications industry and to contribute to a more advanced digital ecosystem. **Indra Mardiatna, Chief Technology Officer at Telkomsel,** stated, "Through this collaboration, Telkomsel and Huawei are jointly driving technological innovation that enhances customer experience and strengthens telecommunications services in Indonesia. With cutting-edge digital telecommunications services, we aspire to progress together with society and industry, bringing us closer to realizing the vision of Golden Indonesia 2045." **Indrawan, Chief Human Capital Officer at Telkomsel,** said, "Beyond technological innovation, Telkomsel is also committed to developing digital talent to be well-prepared for industry changes. With Huawei's support, we aim to create a workforce that understands and implements the latest technologies to drive Indonesia's digital advancement. This initiative aligns with Telkomsel's spirit of accelerating Indonesia's progress, creating a better today, and a brighter future." **Director of Huawei Indonesia Delivery and Service Business Department, Li Bingyao,** noted, "We are honored to collaborate with Telkomsel in a strategic partnership for the development of digital talent. Huawei has always supported and will continue to support Telkomsel's evolution to become the leading Digital Telco in Indonesia through various continuous innovative breakthroughs to provide a convenient and best digital connection experience for all Indonesians and support the future-oriented 5G-Advanced Digital Indonesia Vision.” The Mobile World Congress 2025 takes place from March 3 to March 6 in Barcelona, Spain. Both Telkomsel and Huawei participate alongside other industry stakeholders to explore collective growth opportunities, create new business value, and embrace the era of All Intelligence. For more information, please visit [carrier.huawei.com/en/events/mwc2025](<https://carrier.huawei.com/en/events/mwc2025>).
