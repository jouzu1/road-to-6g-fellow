This volume, **Edge Learning for 6G-Enabled Internet of Things: Challenges, Threats, and Opportunities,** focuses on developing powerful edge-to-edge interaction that allows better endpoint communication, which can be improved to a greater extent with the help of the 6G networks. This allows interaction of cloud services around the world with data centers and enhances the speed and reliability of cloud services and storage. The replication of cloud services and data and mirroring of cloud data would be handled with increased performance and reliability with the edge-level optimization with 6G services. The volume explores 6G-assisted edge learning, energy optimization for edge-assisted 6G networks, applications of 6G-edge-assisted IoT interface, and the data privacy and security challenges of 6G-based-edge systems. It presents case studies of 6G-assisted edge IoT in agricultural science, in healthcare, in transportation, in traffic management, etc. Chapters also address privacy and personalization of 6G-assisted IoT-based edge services, security vulnerabilities in 6G-assisted IoT-based edge services, and more.
