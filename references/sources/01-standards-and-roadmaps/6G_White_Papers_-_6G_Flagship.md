### 6G Visions on paper

#### 6G RESEARCH VISIONS

##### White Paper Series

70 experts imagined the world in 6G. As a result, the first 6G White Paper in the world was born. Since then, 6G Flagship has brought together over 300 experts from more than 30 countries to produce fifteen 6G White Papers, furthering the 6G vision.

##### +300 Experts'

#### Visions on 6G

Since the _world 's first 6G White Paper_ was published in September 2019, the series has become one of the most widely read bodies of 6G research literature, with over a million downloads. Twelve expert groups, 255 participants, 108 organisations. Each group tackled a distinct research challenge, from machine learning and networking to security and societal impact. The papers launched through webinars that drew 805 experts from 67 countries. The latest addition is the 2025 [white paper on 6G resilience](<https://www.6gflagship.com/6g-resilience-white-paper/>). In total, over 300 experts have shaped this series. Click into our visions on 6G below!
