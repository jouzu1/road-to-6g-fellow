### Sionna: An Open-Source Library for 6G Research

Sionna is a GPU-accelerated open-source library for research in communication systems. It is differentiable and features a lightning-fast ray tracer for radio propagation, a versatile link-level simulator, and system-level simulation capabilities. [Get the Code](<https://github.com/nvlabs/sionna>)

#### Key Features

##### Democratize 6G Research

Sionna implements a wide range of carefully tested state-of-the-art algorithms that can be used for rapid prototyping and end-to-end performance evaluation. This allows you to focus on more impactful and reproducible research and frees you from spending time on implementing components outside your area of expertise. Sionna is an essential tool for advancing next-generation communication systems, such as 6G.

##### Everything Is Differentiable

Sionna is built on top of powerful automatic differentiation frameworks, which can backpropagate gradients through an entire communication system. This enables gradient-based optimization and machine learning, particularly the integration of neural networks.

##### Modular and Extensible

Every building block is an independent module that can be easily tested, understood, and modified according to your needs. Sionna offers a high-level Python application programming interface (API) that simplifies the modeling of complex communication systems while ensuring full adaptability to meet your research needs.

##### Jumpstart Your Research

Sionna comes with extensive documentation, including many tutorials that help you get started quickly. Sionna supports NVIDIA GPUs out of the box, which makes it super-fast and perfectly suited for machine learning research in communications.

#### Benefits

###### Native AI Support

The differentiable components of Sionna enable groundbreaking opportunities for research and innovation.

###### Integrated Research Platform

Sionna combines channel-, link-, and system-level simulation capabilities with machine learning and GPU support.

###### Open-Source

Sionna is Python-based, open-source, and welcomes contributions from the community.
