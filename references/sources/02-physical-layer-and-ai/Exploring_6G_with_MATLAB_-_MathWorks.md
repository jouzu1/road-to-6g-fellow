### Exploring 6G with MATLAB

[Read white paper](<https://www.mathworks.com/campaigns/offers/next/exploring-6g-with-matlab.html>) Engineers starting 6G design can accelerate their process of research, development, and testing with MATLAB ® and its wireless communications tools. Read this white paper to learn about 6G applications and use cases and how MATLAB capabilities in the following technology areas can help you bring your 6G vision to reality: * Deep Learning and Artificial Intelligence * Joint Communications and Sensing * Reconfigurable Intelligent Services * Non-Terrestrial Networks * Extreme Data Rates and Higher Frequencies * Physical Layer Design
